## 📋 Project Overview

This Quality Improvement initiative integrates **Internal Medicine residents** into the Rapid Response System (RRS) at Guthrie Lourdes Hospital as core members of a **Medical Emergency Team (MET)**.

### Primary Objectives
- ✅ Improve patient outcomes through early recognition and intervention for deteriorating patients
- ✅ Enhance resident education in acute care management
- ✅ Foster collaborative teamwork between nursing and physician staff

> *"Outcome improvements were most consistently observed when the study period was a minimum of 1 year with accompanying educational efforts. This implies that education and time are necessary to achieve cultural change for meaningful use of an RRT system."*
> — Fischer et al., JAMA 2021

## 👥 Team Composition

| Role | Personnel | Number |
|------|-----------|--------|
| **Resident Team** | IM Residents (PGY-1 to PGY-3) | 3 |
| **ICU Nursing** | ICU RN | 2 |
| **Respiratory** | Respiratory Therapist | 1 |
| **Pharmacy** | Clinical Pharmacist | As available |
| **Supervision** | RRT Coordinator | 1 |

### Resident Roles
| Role | Responsibilities |
|------|-----------------|
| 🎖️ **Team Leader** | Coordinates response, makes clinical decisions, communicates with attending |
| 🩺 **Bedside Assessment** | Rapid ABCD assessment, procedures & interventions |
| 📋 **Chart Review/Orders** | Reviews EMR & history, enters orders, coordinates with teams |

## ⚡ Activation Criteria

### Physiologic Triggers
| Category | Criteria |
|----------|----------|
| **Respiratory** | RR <8 or >28, SpO₂ <90% on O₂, respiratory distress |
| **Circulation** | HR <40 or >130, SBP <90, new arrhythmia |
| **Neurologic** | Acute change in consciousness, new confusion, seizure |
| **Other** | Temp <35°C or >39.5°C, significant bleeding |

### Key Policies
- **"Worried" Criterion:** Anyone can activate the RRT for any reason based on clinical concern
- **Non-Punitive Policy:** No criticism for activation — "over-activation" preferred to delayed activation

## 📈 Quality Metrics

*Based on Third International Consensus Conference on Rapid Response Systems*

- 📉 Cardiac arrests in general ward patients *(primary outcome)*
- ⚠️ Predictable cardiac arrests (deterioration in preceding 24h)
- ⏱️ Timeliness of escalation (trigger to activation)
- 🏃 Time to team arrival at bedside
- 💉 Critical care intervention timeliness
- 📝 Treatment goals/code status documentation
- 📊 RRT activation rate per 1000 admissions
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Patient/family activation utilization
- 🏥 Staff safety culture survey results
